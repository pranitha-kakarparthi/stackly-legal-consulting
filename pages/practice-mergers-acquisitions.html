Transaction Lifecycle

The 4-Stage M&A Execution Protocol

From strategic deal architecture to regulatory closing, our rigorous protocol ensures institutional certainty.

Phase 01

Term Sheet & Non-Disclosure Architecture

Negotiating binding and non-binding heads of terms, exclusivity covenants, non-solicitation restrictions, and break-fee stipulations.

Phase 02

Forensic Legal & Regulatory Due Diligence

Deploying forensic teams across virtual data rooms to analyze corporate records, encumbrances, labor liabilities, and patent ownership.

Phase 03

Definitive Agreement & Antitrust Filings

Drafting Share Purchase Agreements (SPA), Disclosure Letters, and managing statutory filings before the Competition Commission and Sector Regulators.

Phase 04

Escrow Execution & Post-Closing Integration

Managing multi-currency escrow closing protocols, indemnification holdback monitoring, and corporate governance integration.
