Landmark Case Study

The Apex Cross-Border Trade Sanctions Triumph

When our client, a leading global freight aggregator, was inadvertently flagged under newly enacted cross-border export regulations, three container vessels were seized at international ports.

Stackly mobilized an emergency dual-bench litigation squad within 4 hours. By liaising simultaneously with maritime tribunals, trade ministries, and port authorities, we demonstrated statutory exemption under humanitarian transshipment clauses.

Outcome: Complete vessel release within 36 hours with zero statutory fines and unblemished customs classification.
