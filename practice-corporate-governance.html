Methodology

The 4-Stage Governance Assurance Protocol

Our standardized process ensures continuous institutional compliance without interrupting operational velocity.

Phase 01

Fiduciary & Regulatory Gap Audit

Comprehensive forensic audit of current corporate filings, board resolutions, shareholder pacts, and existing compliance registers to uncover latent exposure.

Phase 02

Policy Harmonization & Board Charters

Drafting bespoke Code of Conduct rules, related-party review guidelines, insider trading prevention systems, and D&O indemnification covenants.

Phase 03

Executive Committee Pre-Clearance

Conducting pre-meeting briefing sessions for audit committee members and independent directors before critical cross-border or high-value voting motions.

Phase 04

Real-Time Continuous Inquest Defense

Immediate 4-hour deployment of senior partner representation if statutory authorities issue inquiries, show-cause notices, or demand forensic documents.
