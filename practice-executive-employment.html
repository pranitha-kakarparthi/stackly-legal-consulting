Governance Protocol

The 4-Stage Executive Employment Lifecycle

From C-suite onboarding to confidential post-departure enforcement, our lifecycle ensures absolute corporate protection.

Phase 01

Pre-Hire Agreement Structuring

Drafting bespoke C-level contracts, restrictive covenants, IP assignment deeds, and golden handshake triggers aligned with statutory labour laws.

Phase 02

Continuous Workplace Compliance Audits

Annual inspection of employment handbooks, POSH committees, provident fund registers, and contractor statutory obligations.

Phase 03

Boardroom Exit & Separation Protocol

Executing confidential negotiated transitions, drafting comprehensive release-of-claims waivers, and managing garden leave agreements.

Phase 04

Post-Departure Restrictive Enforcement

Immediate court injunctive action if departing leadership breaches non-solicitation, attempts client poaching, or uses proprietary data.
